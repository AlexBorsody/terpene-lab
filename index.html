Are essential oils fatty oils?

Essential oils aren’t fatty oils like olive or coconut oil. They’re concentrated mixtures of volatile plant compounds, often rich in terpenes and terpenoids.

      The forest isn't silent.

      Plants send chemistry into the air around us. Other plants, insects, animals and humans can receive those molecules.

      Plants have been speaking in chemistry for hundreds of millions of years.

      Other plants respond to these signals. Humans sense them too.

      Plants clearly exchange chemical information with the living world around them. We know humans receive these molecules too, especially through smell. How much of that ancient chemical conversation extends to us is still being worked out.

      “Language” is a metaphor for chemical signaling studied in chemical ecology. It does not imply conscious speech.
