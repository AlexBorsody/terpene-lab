    BEYOND AROMA

    The language of plants.

    Terpenes are more than scent. Plants release volatile compounds into the air and environment as chemical signals — attracting pollinators, deterring threats, responding to damage, and influencing interactions with neighboring plants.

    Humans evolved inside that same chemical world. We detect many of these molecules through smell, and some interact with biological systems in ways researchers are still working to understand.

    Plants have been speaking in chemistry for hundreds of millions of years. We're only beginning to understand the language.

    A metaphor grounded in chemical ecology — not a claim that plants consciously speak.
